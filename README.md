# Omelette Quest

A 2D caveman platformer. Outrun the velociraptors and pterodactyls across 10 levels, climb the volcano, and beat the giant T-Rex to win its egg.

**Play:** https://nugehs.github.io/omelette-quest/

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| ← → / A D | Move |
| Space / W / ↑ | Jump |
| ↓ | Drop through a platform |
| J / X | Swing your club |
| P | Pause |

## Rules

- You have 10 hearts. Raptors and pterodactyls each take one when they hit you.
- Your club does 2 damage; raptors and pterodactyls have 10 hearts each.
- Berries appear now and then and restore 2 hearts.
- Level 11: dodge the T-Rex's charge on the platforms so it crashes into the wall, then club it while it's dazed.

The whole game is one file, `index.html`, with no build step.
